Your next assignment, and how long you have left — in the menu bar.

One purchase. No subscription, no account, no licence key.

It does one thing

Click the menu bar and you get the next few things you owe, colour-coded by how soon they are. One click opens any of them in Canvas. That's the whole app.

No login

It reads the calendar feed Canvas already gives you. Nothing to sign into, no permission needed from your school.

Works offline

Keeps the last list it downloaded and tells you how old it is, instead of going blank on the train.

Never guesses

If it can't work out exactly when something is due, it doesn't show it. A wrong deadline is worse than no deadline.

Invisible until it matters

No Dock icon, no window, one wake-up a minute. Measured at 0.0% CPU and 13 MB.

Google Classroom too

Any iCal link works. Add several and they merge into one list, with duplicates removed.

Reminders, if you want them

24 hours, 3 hours or 1 hour before. Off by default — it never asks unless you turn them on.

What it doesn't do

On purpose.

  • No account, no sign-up, no email address
  • No analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting
  • No subscription and no licence key to lose
  • No planner, no to-do list, no AI assistant

The only network requests it ever makes are to your own calendar link, and to check for a new version when you press the button.

Getting started

Copy one link out of Canvas and paste it in. It takes about a minute — step-by-step instructions here.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel.
